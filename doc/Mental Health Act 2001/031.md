What does the independent consultant psychiatrist do under s.17?

- **Examines** the patient
- **Interviews** the consultant psychiatrist responsible for the patient's care and treatment
- **Reviews the patient's records**
- Decides whether the patient has a mental disorder and **reports in writing within 14 days** to the tribunal, with a copy to the patient's legal representative

The centre must admit them and allow access. Obstructing or failing to co-operate is an **offence**.

*s.17*
