Who decides what? Roles in involuntary admission.

| Role | Decision |
|---|---|
| Registered medical practitioner | Recommendation |
| Consultant psychiatrist (centre) | Admission order, renewal order, discharge, leave |
| Mental Health Commission | Refers to tribunal, assigns legal rep, directs independent psychiatrist |
| Mental Health Tribunal | Affirm or revoke; authorises transfer to CMH and psycho-surgery |
| Circuit Court | Patient's appeal of a tribunal decision |
| District Court | Child orders (s.25); registration appeals |
| Inspector | Inspects centres, reports to Commission |
