What is a person in Garda custody under s.12?

**In Garda custody** awaiting an RMP's decision. Not yet a patient.

- A Garda had **reasonable grounds** to believe a **mental disorder** and a **serious likelihood of immediate and serious harm**
- Taken into custody (premises can be entered **by force**)
- Garda applies **forthwith** to an RMP for a recommendation
- If the RMP **refuses**, they are **released immediately**
- If a **recommendation** is made, a Garda **removes** them to the approved centre

*s.12*
