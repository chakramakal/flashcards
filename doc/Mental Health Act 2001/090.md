What is a patient on s.24?

**Voluntary adult certified for detention.**

- A **second consultant psychiatrist** (not a spouse or relative) examined them and **issued a certificate** (Commission's form) that they should be detained
- The first consultant psychiatrist then makes an **admission order**, so they are an **involuntary patient**
- Sections **15 to 22** apply (renewal, notice, tribunal, appeal, transfer)
- If the second psychiatrist is **not** satisfied, they issue a certificate that the person should **not** be detained and they are **discharged**

*s.24*
