What happens to a patient's status after a tribunal decision?

- **Order affirmed:** the patient stays on the order; they may **appeal to the Circuit Court** within **14 days** of receiving notice
- **Order revoked:** the tribunal directs **discharge**
- The decision, with reasons, goes to the Commission, the consultant psychiatrist, the patient and legal representative
- A **Circuit Court** appeal can **revoke** the order if the patient shows they are not suffering from a mental disorder
