What is a discharged patient under s.28?

**Discharged.** The consultant psychiatrist **revoked the order** because the patient no longer has a mental disorder.

- The patient and legal representative receive a **notice**: they are being discharged and are entitled to have the **tribunal review** completed or held
- Copies go to the **Commission** and, where appropriate, the **health board** and **housing authority**
- A review already started stops **unless the patient asks in writing within 14 days** that it be completed; one not yet started is held only if they ask within 14 days
- **No legal authority to detain** remains; they may be admitted **voluntarily** if they wish
