What can a tribunal decide when reviewing an admission or renewal order?

- **Affirm** the order if satisfied the patient has a mental disorder and the procedural provisions (ss.9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 16) were complied with, or any failure **does not affect the substance** or cause an **injustice**
- Otherwise **revoke** the order and **direct discharge**

It must have regard to the **independent psychiatrist's report**.

*s.18(1), (3)*
