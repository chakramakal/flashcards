What is a patient on a s.14 admission order?

**Involuntary patient: admission order.** The person becomes a **"patient"** (s.14(1)(a)).

- Made by a consultant psychiatrist on the centre's staff after examination, if satisfied of a **mental disorder**
- Authorises **reception, detention and treatment**
- Lasts **21 days**
- Within **24 hours**: copy to the **Commission** and **written notice** to the patient (s.16)
- **Tribunal** reviews within **21 days**; patient has legal representation
- Part 4 rules on **consent to treatment** apply

*ss.14, 15(1), 16, 17, 18*
