What is a patient on leave (s.26)?

**Patient on leave.**

- The **consultant psychiatrist responsible** gave **written permission** to be absent for a period shorter than the unexpired order, with **conditions**
- The patient is still **subject to the order**
- The consultant psychiatrist can **withdraw** permission and **direct the patient in writing to return** if it is in their interests
- Applies to a **child detained under s.25** as well
