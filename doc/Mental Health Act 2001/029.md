What must the written notice to the patient say?

That the patient:

- is being **detained** under s.14 or s.15
- is entitled to **legal representation**
- will be given a **general description of the proposed treatment**
- is entitled to **communicate with the Inspector**
- will have their detention **reviewed by a tribunal**
- has a right of **appeal to the Circuit Court** against a tribunal decision
- may be admitted as a **voluntary patient** if they wish

*s.16(2)*
