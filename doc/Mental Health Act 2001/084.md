What is a voluntary patient?

**Voluntary patient** (s.2, s.29): receiving care in an approved centre with **no admission order or renewal order**.

- Admitted with no application, recommendation or order
- An **adult** who says they want to leave can be **detained up to 24 h** if a consultant psychiatrist, RMP or registered nurse on staff thinks they have a mental disorder (s.23)
- A **child**: if the parents want to remove them and staff think they have a mental disorder, they can be detained and placed in **health board custody** (s.23(2))
- **Seclusion and restraint rules** (s.69) still apply

*ss.2, 23, 29, 69*
