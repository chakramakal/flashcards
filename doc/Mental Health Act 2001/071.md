What can regulations on approved centres cover (s.66)?

Standards for **accommodation, food and care**, including requirements for:

- **maintenance, care and welfare** of residents
- **staffing** and staff suitability
- **premises** (design, cleanliness, ventilation, heating, lighting)
- a **register of residents** and **records** (open to the Inspector)
- an **individual care plan** for each resident, drawn up **in consultation with them** where practicable, with appropriate goals
