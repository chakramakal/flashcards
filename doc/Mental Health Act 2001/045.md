What special protections apply to a child detained under s.25?

- **Psycho-surgery** and **ECT** need **court approval**
- **Medicine for more than 3 months** needs approval by the responsible consultant psychiatrist **and** authorisation by a second consultant psychiatrist (s.61)
- Section 25 children are covered by the rules on **leave** (s.26), **absence without leave** (s.27) and **seclusion and restraint** (s.69)

*ss.25(12)-(13), 26(3), 27(3), 61, 69(4)*
