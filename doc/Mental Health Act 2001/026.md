How long does an admission order last and how is it renewed?

- Admission order: **21 days**
- 1st renewal: up to **3 months**
- 2nd renewal: up to **6 months**
- Further renewals: up to **12 months** each

Renewal orders are made by the **consultant psychiatrist responsible** for the patient's care and treatment.

*s.15(1)-(3)*
