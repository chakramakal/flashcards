After a voluntary adult patient is detained under s.23, what must happen?

The consultant psychiatrist responsible must either:

- **discharge** the person, or
- arrange an **examination by another consultant psychiatrist** (not a spouse or relative)

If the second psychiatrist is satisfied of a mental disorder, they issue a **certificate** (Commission's form) that the person should be detained, and the first psychiatrist makes an **admission order**. If not, the person is **discharged**. Sections 15-22 then apply as for a s.14 admission.
