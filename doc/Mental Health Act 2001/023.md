Who arranges removal to the approved centre after a recommendation (non-Garda route)?

- The **applicant** arranges it
- If unable, the **clinical director** (or a consultant psychiatrist acting for them) arranges removal by **centre staff**, at the RMP's request
- If both are of opinion there is a **serious likelihood of immediate and serious harm**, they may request the **Gardaí** to assist; Gardaí may enter premises by force and take reasonable measures including **detention or restraint**

*s.13*
