Who sits on a Mental Health Tribunal?

**Three members**, decided by majority vote:

- a **consultant psychiatrist**
- a **barrister or solicitor** with at least 7 years' experience, who is the **chairperson**
- a **third person** who is none of the above, nor an RMP or registered nurse

Commission members are **disqualified** from sitting on a tribunal.
