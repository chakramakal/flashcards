Order the steps when a voluntary adult patient wants to leave and is detained.

- 1 Patient indicates they want to leave
- 2 Consultant psychiatrist, RMP or registered nurse on staff forms the opinion they have a **mental disorder**
- 3 Patient **detained up to 24 h**
- 4 Responsible consultant psychiatrist either **discharges** or arranges a **second consultant psychiatrist's examination**
- 5 If satisfied, second psychiatrist issues a **certificate to detain**; first psychiatrist makes an **admission order**
- 6 Sections 15-22 apply (renewal, notice, tribunal, appeal)
