How are approved centres registered?

- The Commission keeps a public **Register of Approved Centres**
- Registration lasts **3 years**
- The registered proprietor may reapply **at least 2 months** before it expires
- The Commission may **refuse or remove** a centre only on limited grounds (premises or running not meeting regulations, conviction under Part 5, false or withheld information, breach of a condition)
- It must notify the proposal with reasons; the proprietor has **21 days** to make representations
