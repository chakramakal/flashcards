Define "mental disorder" (s.3).

Mental illness, severe dementia or significant intellectual disability, **and either**:

- **(a) Risk:** because of it, a serious likelihood of causing **immediate and serious harm** to self or others, **or**
- **(b) Impaired judgment (both limbs needed):**
    - (i) judgment so impaired that not admitting would likely cause **serious deterioration** or prevent **treatment that can only be given by admission**, **and**
    - (ii) admission, detention and treatment would likely **benefit or alleviate** the condition to a **material extent**

*s.3(1)*
