A voluntary patient is a child and the parents want to remove them. What happens?

- If the consultant psychiatrist, RMP or registered nurse thinks the child has a **mental disorder**, the child may be **detained** and placed in the **custody of the health board**
- The health board must either **return the child** to the parents (or person acting *in loco parentis*) or apply under **s.25** at the next **District Court** sitting, or a specially arranged one **within 3 days**
- The health board keeps custody pending the hearing

*s.23(2)-(3)*
