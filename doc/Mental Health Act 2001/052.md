What specific duties does the Commission have?

- **Appoint tribunal members** and provide staff and facilities for tribunals
- Establish a **panel of consultant psychiatrists** for independent examinations (s.17)
- Make schemes for **legal aid** to patients
- **Advise the Minister**
- Prepare **codes of practice** for people working in mental health services
- Issue **guidelines** for approved-centre staff on s.8 (criteria for admission)
- **Register** approved centres and make **rules** (ECT, seclusion and restraint)

*ss.8(3), 33(3), 59, 64, 69*
