Order the steps of involuntary admission (adult, community route).

- 1 **Application** to a registered medical practitioner (RMP)
- 2 RMP **examines** within 24 h and makes a **recommendation** (valid 7 days)
- 3 Removal to the approved centre
- 4 **Consultant psychiatrist** on the centre's staff examines and makes an **admission order** (21 days)
- 5 Copy to the Commission and written notice to the patient within 24 h
- 6 Commission refers to a **tribunal**, assigns a legal representative, and directs an **independent psychiatrist** to report within 14 days
- 7 Tribunal decides within 21 days of the order
