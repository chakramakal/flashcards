What are a patient's procedural rights at a tribunal?

- **Notice** of the date, time and place of the sitting
- A **copy of the s.17 report** and an indication of the nature and source of any other information that came to notice
- To be **present** (unless attendance is prejudicial) and to present their case **in person or through a legal representative**
- To **cross-examine** witnesses called by the tribunal and to call their own

*s.49(6), (11)*
