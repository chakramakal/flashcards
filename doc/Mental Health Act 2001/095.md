What is a patient transferred to another approved centre?

**Transferred to another approved centre** (s.20 or s.21).

- Arranged by the **clinical director**, with the consent of the **other centre's clinical director**
- Because the patient or applicant asked (**s.20**), or it benefits the patient or is needed for **special treatment** (**s.21**)
- The sending clinical director gives **notice to the Commission**
- Detention counts as detention **in the centre they were transferred from**
- Detained until the **order expires** (s.20(3))

*ss.20, 21*
