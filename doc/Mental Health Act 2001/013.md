Who counts as a "relative" and a "spouse"?

- **Relative:** parent, grandparent, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, niece, nephew or child of the person or of their spouse (whole blood, half blood or by affinity)
- **Spouse:** husband or wife, or a cohabiting partner of the opposite sex for **3 years or more** (as worded in the enacted Act)
- Spouse does **not** include one living separately and apart, or where a Domestic Violence Act order exists (s.9(8))
