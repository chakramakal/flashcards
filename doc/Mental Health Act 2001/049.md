What must be given or sent when a patient is discharged under s.28?

- **Notice** to the patient and their legal representative that they are being discharged and are entitled to have the tribunal review completed or held
- **Copies** of the order and notice to the **Commission** and, where appropriate, the **health board** and **housing authority**
- A tribunal review already started is **discontinued** unless the patient asks in writing, **within 14 days**, for it to be completed; a review not yet started is held only if the patient asks within 14 days

*s.28(3)-(5)*
