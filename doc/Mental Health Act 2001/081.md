Compare the community route and the Garda route to involuntary admission.

| | Community (s.9) | Garda (s.12) |
|---|---|---|
| Who starts | Spouse, relative, authorised officer, Garda, other | Garda only |
| Grounds | Mental disorder | Mental disorder **and** serious likelihood of immediate and serious harm |
| Person's status meanwhile | Not in custody | **In Garda custody** |
| Application | To RMP, within 48 h of observing | **Forthwith** to RMP |
| If RMP refuses | No admission | **Released immediately** |
| Removal | Applicant (or centre staff via clinical director) | **Garda removes** |
