Key time limits: adult involuntary admission.

| Step | Limit | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Applicant observed person | within 48 h before application | 9(4) |
| RMP examination | within 24 h of application | 10(2) |
| Recommendation valid | 7 days | 10(5) |
| Detain pending admission exam | up to 24 h | 14(2) |
| Admission order | 21 days | 15(1) |
| 1st / 2nd / later renewals | 3 months / 6 months / 12 months | 15 |
| Certify before renewal | within 1 week | 15(4) |
| Copy to Commission, notice to patient | within 24 h | 16 |
| Independent psychiatrist report | 14 days | 17 |
| Tribunal decision | 21 days (extendable in 14-day steps) | 18 |
| Appeal to Circuit Court | 14 days | 19 |
