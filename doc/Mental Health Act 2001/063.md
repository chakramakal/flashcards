What is required before psycho-surgery?

- The patient's **written consent**, **and**
- **Authorisation by a tribunal** (after the consultant psychiatrist notifies the Commission, which refers it) as being in the patient's **best interests of health**
- Cannot take effect until the **appeal period** has passed or any appeal is decided

Psycho-surgery means surgery that **destroys brain tissue or its functioning**, performed to ameliorate a mental disorder.
