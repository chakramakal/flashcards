When can a clinical director transfer a patient (s.21), and what about the Central Mental Hospital?

- **To another approved centre:** if it benefits the patient or is needed for **special treatment**, with the consent of that centre's clinical director
- **To the Central Mental Hospital:** the clinical director notifies the **Commission**, which refers the proposal to a **tribunal**. The tribunal reviews it within **14 days** and authorises it only if in the **best interest of the patient's health**. It cannot take effect until the **appeal period** has passed or any appeal is decided.
- Notice of transfer to the Commission; detention is treated as in the original centre
