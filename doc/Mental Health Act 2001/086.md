What is a person held under s.14(2)?

**Held for admission examination.** Not yet a patient.

- Arrived at the approved centre with a **recommendation**
- A **consultant psychiatrist, medical practitioner or registered nurse** on staff can **take charge** of the person and **detain them for up to 24 hours**
- Ends when an **admission order is made or refused**, within that 24 hours
- The consultant psychiatrist who examines must not be a spouse or relative, or the applicant
