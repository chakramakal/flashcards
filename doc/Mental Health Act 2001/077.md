What roles does a registered nurse have under the Act?

- May **take charge of and detain** a person for up to **24 h** pending the admission examination (s.14(2))
- May **detain a voluntary patient** for up to **24 h** who wants to leave, if of opinion they have a mental disorder (s.23)
- **2 psychiatric nurses** sit on the Mental Health Commission (s.35)
- A registered nurse **cannot** be the third (lay) member of a tribunal (s.48(3)(c))

*ss.14, 23, 35, 48*
