When and how does the RMP make a recommendation?

- RMP examines the person **within 24 hours** of receiving the application
- Must **tell the person the purpose** of the examination, unless this might harm their mental health, well-being or emotional condition
- If **satisfied** the person has a mental disorder, makes a **recommendation** in the Commission's form naming an approved centre (**not the Central Mental Hospital**)

*s.10(1)-(2)*
