What is a patient on s.23(1)?

**Voluntary adult detained** (up to 24 hours).

- A **voluntary adult patient** said they wish to leave
- A **consultant psychiatrist, RMP or registered nurse** on staff is of opinion they have a **mental disorder**
- They may be **detained up to 24 hours**
- The responsible consultant psychiatrist must then **discharge** them or arrange a **second consultant psychiatrist's examination** (s.24)

*ss.23(1), 24(1)*
