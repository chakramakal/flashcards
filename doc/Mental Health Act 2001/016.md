Who may apply for an adult's involuntary admission?

An application for a recommendation is made to a **registered medical practitioner** by:

- the person's **spouse or a relative**
- an **authorised officer** (health board officer of a prescribed rank, authorised by the chief executive officer)
- a **member of the Garda Síochána**
- **any other person** (must state reasons, their connection to the person, and the circumstances)

*s.9(1), (5), (8)*
