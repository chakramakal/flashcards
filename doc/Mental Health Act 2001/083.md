Legal statuses under the Mental Health Act 2001: what is each section called?

The Act describes these situations but does not give them names. These are working labels.

| Section | Status | Length |
|---|---|---|
| s.29 | **Voluntary patient** | Until they leave (adults can be held 24 h under s.23) |
| s.12 | **In Garda custody** awaiting an RMP | Until a recommendation is made or refused |
| s.14(2) | **Held for admission examination** | Up to 24 h |
| s.14 | **Involuntary patient: admission order** | 21 days |
| s.15 | **Involuntary patient: renewal order** | 3 months, then 6, then 12 |
| s.23(1) | **Voluntary adult detained** | Up to 24 h |
| s.24 | **Voluntary adult certified for detention** | Admission order follows |
| s.23(2) | **Child held in health board custody** | Until returned or s.25 application (within 3 days) |
| s.25 | **Child detained by court order** | 21 days, then 3 months, then 6 |
| s.26 | **Patient on leave** | As the written permission says |
| s.27 | **Patient absent without leave** | Until returned |
| s.20, s.21 | **Transferred to another approved centre** | Order continues |
| s.21(2) | **Transferred to the Central Mental Hospital** | Order continues |
| s.22 | **Transferred to a hospital or other place** | As long as needed for treatment |
| s.28 | **Discharged** | Order revoked |
