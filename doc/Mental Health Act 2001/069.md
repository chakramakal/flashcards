What conditions can the Commission attach to a centre's registration?

- Essential **maintenance or refurbishment**
- **Closure** of areas
- **Maximum number of residents**
- **Minimum number of staff**
- Introduction or review of **care and welfare policies**
- Introduction or review of **medicine policies** (ordering, prescribing, storing, administering)
- Measures to ensure patients and residents are **told their rights** under the Act

Breaching a condition is an **offence**.

*s.64(6), (13)*
