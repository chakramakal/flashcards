What is a patient absent without leave (s.27)?

**Absent without leave.** A patient with an admission order, renewal order or s.25 order who:

- **left without permission**, or
- **failed to return** when directed or when leave ended, or
- **failed to comply with a condition** of leave

The **clinical director** can arrange for **centre staff** to bring them back. If there is a **serious likelihood of immediate and serious harm**, the clinical director (or a consultant psychiatrist for them) may ask the **Gardaí**, who can enter premises by force and take reasonable measures, including **detention or restraint**.
