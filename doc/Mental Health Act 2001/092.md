What is a child on a s.25 order?

**Child detained by court order.**

- Ordered by the **District Court** on the **health board's** application: mental disorder **and** needs treatment they are unlikely to get without an order
- **21 days**, then extension of up to **3 months**, then **6 months** at a time (each extension needs a fresh examination)
- **Psycho-surgery** and **ECT** need **court approval**; medicine over **3 months** needs a second consultant psychiatrist's authorisation (s.61)
- **Leave** (s.26), **absence without leave** (s.27) and **seclusion/restraint** rules (s.69) apply
- Reviewed by the **court**, not a tribunal

*ss.25, 26, 27, 61, 69*
