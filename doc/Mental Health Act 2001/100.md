Which body reviews each status under the Mental Health Act?

| Status | Reviewing body |
|---|---|
| s.14 admission order | **Mental Health Tribunal** (within 21 days) |
| s.15 renewal order | **Mental Health Tribunal** |
| s.24 certified voluntary adult (after order) | **Mental Health Tribunal** |
| s.21(2) transferred to the CMH | **Mental Health Tribunal** |
| s.25 child | **District Court** |
| Voluntary patient | None; can leave, subject to s.23 |
| Patients under the Criminal Law (Insanity) Act 2006 | **Review Board** (not the Tribunal) |
