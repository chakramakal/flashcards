Who can appeal a tribunal decision, to where, and how quickly?

- Only the **patient**, against a decision to **affirm** an order, on the ground that they are **not suffering from a mental disorder**
- To the **Circuit Court**, by written notice within **14 days** of receiving notice of the decision
- Heard by the judge of the circuit where the **centre** is, or (at the patient's option) where they are **ordinarily resident**

*s.19(1)-(3)*
