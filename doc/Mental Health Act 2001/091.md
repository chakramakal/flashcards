What is a child on s.23(2)?

**Child held in health board custody.**

- A **voluntary** child patient whose parents (or person acting *in loco parentis*) want to remove them
- A consultant psychiatrist, RMP or registered nurse thinks they have a **mental disorder**
- Detained and placed in the **custody of the health board**
- The board must **return the child** or apply under **s.25** at the next **District Court** sitting, or a specially arranged one **within 3 days**
- The board keeps custody until the hearing
