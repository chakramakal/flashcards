Who is disqualified from making an application?

- A person **under 18**
- An authorised officer or Garda who is a **relative** of the person or their spouse
- A **governing body member, staff member or person in charge** of the approved centre concerned
- Anyone with an **interest in the payments** for the person's care in that centre
- An RMP who provides a **regular medical service** at that centre
- The **spouse or close relative** of any of the above
