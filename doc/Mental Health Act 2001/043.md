What are the grounds for involuntary admission of a child (s.25)?

The **health board** may apply to the **District Court** if a child in its area:

- is suffering from a **mental disorder**, **and**
- requires **treatment** they are unlikely to receive unless an order is made

A **consultant psychiatrist** (not a relative) must have **examined the child** and a report furnished to the court. If the parents refuse consent to examination or cannot be found, the application can go ahead and the court can direct an examination.

*s.25(1)-(5)*
