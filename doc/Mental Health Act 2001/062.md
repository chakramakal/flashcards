When can treatment be given without consent?

Consent is required **except** where the **responsible consultant psychiatrist** is of opinion the treatment is **necessary** to safeguard life, restore health, alleviate the condition or relieve suffering, **and** the patient is **incapable of giving consent because of their mental disorder**.

This does **not** apply to psycho-surgery, ECT or long-term medicine (ss.58-60).
