What powers does a tribunal have?

- Receive **submissions and evidence**
- **Direct** the responsible consultant psychiatrist to arrange for the patient to **attend**
- **Direct witnesses** to attend and produce documents
- Give other **reasonable and just directions**
- Evidence may be taken **on oath**
- Sittings are held **in private**
- A patient need **not attend** if attendance might prejudice their mental health, well-being or emotional condition
