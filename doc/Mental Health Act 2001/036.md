How does the Circuit Court deal with an appeal?

- Court **affirms** the order **unless the patient shows** to its satisfaction that they are **not** suffering from a mental disorder; if shown, it **revokes** the order
- Hearing is **restricted** (court officers, people directly concerned, bona fide press, others the court permits)
- Nothing that could **identify the patient** may be published or broadcast
- Further appeal to the **High Court** on a **point of law only**

*s.19(4), (8)-(9), (16)*
