What are the formal requirements for an application?

- In the **form specified by the Commission**
- The applicant must have **observed the person within 48 hours** before making it
- A **false or misleading statement** is an offence
- If a further application follows a **refused** one, the applicant must disclose the earlier refusal to the new RMP (s.11)
