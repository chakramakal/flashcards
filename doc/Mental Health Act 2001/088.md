What is a patient on a renewal order?

**Involuntary patient: renewal order** (s.15).

- 1st renewal: up to **3 months**
- 2nd: up to **6 months**
- Further: up to **12 months** each
- Made by the **consultant psychiatrist responsible**, who must have **examined the patient within the previous week** and **certified** they continue to have a mental disorder
- Each renewal order is copied to the **Commission** and goes to a **tribunal** for review

*ss.15(2)-(4), 16, 17*
