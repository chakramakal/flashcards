What is an admission order, and who makes it?

On receiving the recommendation, a **consultant psychiatrist on the staff of the centre** examines the person as soon as may be and either:

- makes an **involuntary admission order** (Commission's form) if satisfied they have a mental disorder, authorising **reception, detention and treatment**; the person then becomes a **"patient"**, **or**
- **refuses** to make the order

The consultant psychiatrist must not be a **spouse or relative** of the person, or the **applicant**.

*s.14(1), (3)*
