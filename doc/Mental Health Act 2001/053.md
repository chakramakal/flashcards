Who sits on the Mental Health Commission?

**13 members** appointed by the Minister, including a lawyer (10+ years), medical practitioners (2 of 3 consultant psychiatrists), **2 psychiatric nurses**, a social worker, a psychologist, a representative of the general public, 3 voluntary-body representatives (at least 2 with lived experience of mental illness), and a health board chief executive. At least 4 women and 4 men.
