What can a Garda do under s.12?

If a Garda has **reasonable grounds** to believe a person has a mental disorder **and** there is a **serious likelihood of immediate and serious harm** to self or others, they may:

- **take the person into custody**
- **enter any dwelling or premises by force if need be**

They must apply **forthwith** to an RMP for a recommendation. If the RMP **refuses**, the person is **released immediately**. If a recommendation is made, a Garda **removes** the person to the approved centre.

*s.12*
