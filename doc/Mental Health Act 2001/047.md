When is a patient "absent without leave", and what can be done (s.27)?

A patient with an admission, renewal or s.25 order who:

- **leaves without permission**, or
- **fails to return** when directed or when leave ends, or
- **fails to comply with a condition** of leave

The **clinical director** may arrange for **centre staff** to bring them back. If that is not possible and there is a **serious likelihood of immediate and serious harm**, the clinical director (or a consultant psychiatrist acting for them) may request the **Gardaí**, who may enter premises by force and take reasonable measures including **detention or restraint**.

*s.27*
