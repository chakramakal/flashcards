How long does a s.25 order last, and can it be extended?

- Initial order: up to **21 days**
- First extension: up to **3 months**
- Further extensions: up to **6 months** each
- Each extension needs a **fresh examination** by a consultant psychiatrist (not a relative) and the court must be satisfied the child **still has a mental disorder**
- Urgent applications may be made **ex parte**

*s.25(6)-(11)*
