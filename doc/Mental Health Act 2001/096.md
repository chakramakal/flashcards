What is a patient transferred to the Central Mental Hospital under the MHA?

**Transferred to the Central Mental Hospital** (s.21(2)).

- Still a **patient under the 2001 Act**
- The clinical director notified the **Commission**, which referred the proposal to a **tribunal**
- The tribunal reviewed it **within 14 days** and authorised it as being in the **best interest of the patient's health**
- Not effective until the **appeal period** passed or any appeal was decided
- Detention counts as detention in the **original centre**
- Reviewed by the **Mental Health Tribunal**, not the Review Board

*s.21(2)-(4)*
