What applies when medicine has been given for 3 months?

After a **continuous 3 months** of medicine for the mental disorder, it cannot continue unless:

- the patient gives **written consent**, **or**
- if **unable or unwilling**: it is **approved by the responsible consultant psychiatrist** and **authorised by a second consultant psychiatrist** (Commission's form)

The consent or approval and authorisation lasts **3 months**, then must be renewed every **3 months**.

*s.60*
