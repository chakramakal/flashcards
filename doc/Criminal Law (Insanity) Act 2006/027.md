Who sits on the Review Board?

- A **chairperson** with at least **10 years' experience** as a practising barrister or solicitor, or a **current or former Circuit, High or Supreme Court judge**
- Other members as the Minister decides, including **at least one approved medical officer**
- **5-year** term, eligible for re-appointment
- Decisions by **majority vote**; the chairperson has a **casting vote**
