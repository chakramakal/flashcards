What is a patient on s.5(2)?

**NGRI: committed.**

- Found **not guilty by reason of insanity** (a **special verdict**, not a conviction)
- Court satisfied they have a **mental disorder** (2001 Act meaning) and need **in-patient** care, so **committed** to a designated centre **until an order is made under s.13**
- **Review Board** reviews at least every **6 months**; patient may apply
- When the clinical director thinks in-patient care is no longer needed, they notify the **Board**, which decides on **further care or discharge** (unconditional, or conditional on out-patient treatment or supervision)
- It is **not** a tribunal case
- Leave needs the **Minister's consent**; **Part 4 MHA** governs consent to treatment
