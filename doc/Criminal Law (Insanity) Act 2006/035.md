What if an NGRI patient no longer needs in-patient care (s.13(6)-(7))?

For a patient detained under **s.5**: the clinical director **forthwith notifies the Review Board**, which brings the patient before it, hears the **responsible consultant psychiatrist**, decides whether in-patient treatment is **still required**, and may order **further detention/care/treatment** or **discharge** (unconditional, or on conditions for **out-patient treatment or supervision or both**).
