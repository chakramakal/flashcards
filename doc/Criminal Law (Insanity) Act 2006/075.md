What is a civil patient transferred to the CMH?

**Civil patient transferred to the CMH** (MHA 2001 s.21(2)).

- Still a **patient under the Mental Health Act 2001**, on an **admission or renewal order**
- The clinical director notified the **Commission**, which referred the proposal to a **tribunal**; the tribunal reviewed it within **14 days** and authorised transfer as being in the **best interest of the patient's health**
- Could not take effect until the **appeal period** had passed or any appeal was decided
- Detention counts as detention **in the original centre** (s.21(4))
- Reviewed by the **Mental Health Tribunal**, not the Review Board
