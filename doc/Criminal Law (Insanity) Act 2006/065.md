What is the difference between a patient on s.4 and a patient on s.5?

| | s.4: unfit to be tried | s.5: NGRI |
|---|---|---|
| The finding | Cannot understand the proceedings | Did the act but **not responsible** at the time |
| The charge | **Still outstanding** | **Ended** by the special verdict |
| If they recover | Return to the **committing court** for trial | Board decides on **further care or discharge** |
| Reviewed by | Review Board | Review Board |
