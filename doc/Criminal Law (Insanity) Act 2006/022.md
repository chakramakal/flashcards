Who can appeal a finding of unfitness, and to where?

- **District Court** finding of unfitness: appeal to the **Circuit Court**
- **Central Criminal Court, Circuit Court or Special Criminal Court** finding: appeal to the **Court of Criminal Appeal**
- **No appeal to the Supreme Court**
- If the appeal is allowed, the person is **tried or retried**; if the fitness question was deferred and they ought to have been acquitted first, they are **acquitted**
