What is a patient on s.15(5)(a)?

**Transferred prisoner kept after refusing treatment.**

- Was transferred **with consent** under s.15(1) but **now refuses** care or treatment
- **2 or more relevant officers** certify they should **remain** for treatment, so they **stay**
- The clinical director gives the prisoner a **copy of the certification** and sends one to the Minister
- The **Minister** may direct a **Board review** in the interests of justice
- If no such certification, they are **transferred back to prison** (s.15(5)(b))

*ss.15(5)-(6), 17(1)*
