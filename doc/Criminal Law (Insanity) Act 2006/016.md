What is the test for "not guilty by reason of insanity" (NGRI)?

The accused **committed the act**, and after hearing a **consultant psychiatrist**, the court or jury finds that **at the time**:

- they were suffering from a **mental disorder**, **and**
- it was such that they **ought not to be held responsible** because they:
    - **did not know the nature and quality** of the act, **or**
    - **did not know it was wrong**, **or**
    - were **unable to refrain** from committing it

The verdict is a **special verdict**: "not guilty by reason of insanity".

*s.5(1)*
