Can a patient apply for a review, and can the Board review on its own initiative?

- A patient detained under **s.4** (unfit) or **s.5** (NGRI) may **apply** to the Review Board for a review of their detention
- The Board must hold it unless satisfied it is **not necessary** because of a review already undertaken
- For an **unfit** patient the Board may also find them **no longer unfit**, in which case they go back before the **committing court**
- The Board may also review **on its own initiative**
