What is the difference between s.15(1) and s.15(2)?

| | s.15(1) | s.15(2) |
|---|---|---|
| Consent | Prisoner **consents** | **Not needed**: unwilling or unable |
| Certification | **One** relevant officer | **Two or more** relevant officers |
| If they later refuse | Treated under s.15(5) | Already without consent |
| Minister may direct Board review | Only after s.15(5)(a) | **Yes**, in the interests of justice (s.17(1)) |
