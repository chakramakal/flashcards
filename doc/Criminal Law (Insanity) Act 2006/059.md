Order the steps from a finding of unfitness to review (accused charged with an offence).

- 1 Question of **fitness** raised (defence, prosecution or court)
- 2 Court decides: unable to understand the proceedings (plead, instruct, defend, understand evidence)
- 3 If **unfit** and in need of in-patient care, court **commits** to a designated centre until a **s.13 order** (up to 14 days' committal for examination if unclear)
- 4 Centre cares for the patient; **Review Board** reviews at least every **6 months**
- 5 Clinical director notifies the **court** if the patient becomes fit, or the **Board** if still unfit but no longer needing in-patient care
- 6 Court resumes proceedings, **or** Board orders further care, or discharge (unconditional, or conditional on out-patient care or supervision)
