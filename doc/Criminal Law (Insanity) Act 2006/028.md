What powers does the Review Board have in a review?

- Hold **sittings** and receive submissions and evidence
- Take account of the **court record** of the relevant court
- **Assign a legal representative** to the patient unless they engage one
- **Direct** the responsible consultant psychiatrist to arrange for the patient to **attend**
- **Direct witnesses** to attend and produce documents
- Take evidence **on oath**
- Sittings held **in private**

*s.12(1)-(2), (8)*
