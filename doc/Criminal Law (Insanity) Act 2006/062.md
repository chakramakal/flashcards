What is a patient on an out-patient order under s.4?

**Unfit to be tried: out-patient order.** Applies under **s.4(3)(b)(ii)** or **s.4(5)(c)(ii)**.

- The court was satisfied the person has a mental disorder and needs **out-patient** care or treatment in a designated centre
- The court makes **such order as it thinks proper** for out-patient treatment, so the **terms are in the order itself**
- **Not detained in-patient**
- The charge is still outstanding; the proceedings are adjourned

The Act text does not give further detail on review of these orders, so follow the order's terms and ask the CNM.

*s.4(3)(b)(ii), (5)(c)(ii)*
