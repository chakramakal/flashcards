What paperwork goes with a prisoner transfer direction?

The governor must cause:

- the **original direction** to go to the **clinical director** of the designated centre
- a **copy given to the prisoner before** the transfer
- a **copy sent to the Minister**
- where s.15(2) applies, the **certification** to accompany each

A direction is sufficient authority for the transfer.
