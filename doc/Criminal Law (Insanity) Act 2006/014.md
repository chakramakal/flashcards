Can the court commit someone for examination before deciding (s.4(6))?

Yes. To decide whether to commit or make an order, the court may **commit the accused to a designated centre for up to 14 days** and direct examination by an **approved medical officer** there. The AMO reports within that period on whether the person has a mental disorder and needs **in-patient** or **out-patient** care.
