What is a patient on s.15(1)?

**Prisoner transferred with consent.**

- A **remand or sentenced prisoner** with a **mental disorder** that cannot be appropriately treated in the prison
- A **relevant officer** (approved medical officer or registered medical practitioner) **certified** this in writing
- The prisoner **voluntarily consented** and the **prison governor** directed transfer in writing
- A **copy of the direction** is given to the prisoner **before** transfer
- **Still a prisoner**; the governor tells the clinical director the date they will cease to be one (s.16)
- **Review Board** reviews at least every **6 months**; the prisoner may apply
- When the clinical director says in-patient care is no longer needed, they are **transferred back to prison** (s.18)
