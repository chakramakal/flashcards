Does the Act apply to court-martial and infanticide cases?

- **Defence Forces:** the Defence Act 1954 was amended (ss.202, 203, 203A) so **courts martial** use the same tests for unfitness, NGRI and diminished responsibility
- **Infanticide:** "mental disorder" replaces "effect of lactation", and a woman found guilty may be dealt with as for **manslaughter on the ground of diminished responsibility**
