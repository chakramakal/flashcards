What must the prison governor tell the clinical director (s.16)?

As soon as practicable, **written notice of the date, if known, on which the prisoner will cease to be a prisoner**, and of any change to that date. After that date, nothing in the Act prevents **voluntary or involuntary admission under the Mental Health Act 2001** (or other enactment), and steps to assess this can be taken **beforehand**.

*s.16*
