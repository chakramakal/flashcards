When is a patient "unlawfully at large", and who can arrest them?

A patient on temporary release is **unlawfully at large** if the **period has expired** or a **condition has been broken**.

- A **Garda shall**, and an **officer or servant of the designated centre may**, **arrest without warrant** anyone suspected to be unlawfully at large and bring them back
- If they are arrested, or **return voluntarily**, after breaching a condition, the release period is **deemed to have expired**

*s.14(5)-(7)*
