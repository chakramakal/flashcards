A patient is described as "on a 15(2)". What does that tell you?

- A **prisoner** (remand or sentenced), **not a court-committed patient**
- Transferred **without consent**, on the certification of **2 or more relevant officers**, by the **prison governor's direction**
- Detained in the designated centre under the **2006 Act**, reviewed by the **Review Board** at least every **6 months**
- Goes **back to prison** under s.18 when no longer in need of in-patient care

*ss.15(2), 17, 18*
