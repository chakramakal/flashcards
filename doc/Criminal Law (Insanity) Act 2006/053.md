How did the Act treat people already detained under the older law (s.20)?

- Someone detained under s.17 of the **Lunacy (Ireland) Act 1821** is treated as if detained under a **s.4** order (unfit)
- Someone found **guilty but insane** and detained under s.2 of the **Trial of Lunatics Act 1883** is treated as if detained under a **s.5** order (NGRI)
