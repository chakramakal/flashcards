What is a patient on s.4(6) or s.5(3)?

**Committed for examination.** A short-term committal to a designated centre so an **approved medical officer** can examine the person and report to the court.

- **s.4(6):** accused whose fitness is being decided. Up to **14 days**.
- **s.5(3):** person found NGRI who **may** need in-patient care. Up to **14 days**, extendable on application after consulting an AMO, but **no more than 6 months in total**.
- The AMO reports whether they have a **mental disorder** and need **in-patient** (or, for s.4(6), out-patient) care

*ss.4(6), 5(3)*
