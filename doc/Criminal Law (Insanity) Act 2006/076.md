What is a court-martial patient (Defence Act ss.202-203)?

**Court-martial unfit / NGRI, committed.** The 2006 Act substituted ss.202, 203 and 203A of the **Defence Act 1954** so courts martial follow the same tests.

- **s.202:** found **unfit** to take their trial; **committed** to a designated centre until an order under s.13
- **s.203:** found **not guilty by reason of insanity**; **committed** after consulting the clinical director
- **Review Board** reviews the detention; if the patient is no longer unfit, the Board notifies a **convening authority**, which convenes a **court-martial**
- The **Minister for Defence** may be heard at the Board

*2006 Act ss.12(6)(e), 13, 21*
