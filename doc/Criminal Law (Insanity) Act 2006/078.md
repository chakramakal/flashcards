Who can authorise leave or release, by status?

| Status | Who authorises |
|---|---|
| s.4 / s.5 / s.15 (designated centre) | **Clinical director**, with the **Minister's consent** (s.14(1)) |
| MHA 2001 civil patient (including transferred to the CMH) | **Consultant psychiatrist responsible**, in writing (MHA s.26) |
