Can the fitness question be put off, and can an unfit person still be acquitted?

- On trial the court may **defer** the question until any time **before the opening of the defence case**. If the accused is acquitted before the question falls to be decided, it is **not decided** and they are **acquitted**.
- After a finding of unfitness, the court may on application hear evidence on whether the accused **did the act**. If there is a **reasonable doubt**, it must **order discharge**.
- If the court decides **not** to discharge, publishing a report of that evidence or decision before the trial concludes (or is dropped) is an **offence**.

*s.4(7)-(10)*
