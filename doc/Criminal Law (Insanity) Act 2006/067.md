What is a patient on s.15(2)?

**Prisoner transferred without consent.**

- **2 or more relevant officers** certified in writing that the prisoner has a mental disorder they cannot be treated for in the prison
- The governor directed transfer **even though the prisoner was unwilling or unable to consent**
- **Review Board** reviews at least every **6 months**; the prisoner may apply; the **Minister** may also direct a review in the interests of justice
- Returned to prison under **s.18** when no longer needing in-patient care

*ss.15(2), 17, 18*
