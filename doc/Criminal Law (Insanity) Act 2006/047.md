What if a transferred prisoner refuses treatment (s.15(5))?

- If **2 or more relevant officers certify** they have a mental disorder for which they should remain in the centre for care or treatment, they **remain**
- In any other case they are **transferred back** to the prison they came from, or to **another prison** the Minister considers appropriate
- The clinical director must give the prisoner a **copy of the certification** as soon as practicable and send a copy to the Minister
