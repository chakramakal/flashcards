What is diminished responsibility (s.6)?

On a murder trial, if the jury (or Special Criminal Court) finds the person:

- **did the act**
- was **suffering from a mental disorder** at the time, and
- the disorder was **not enough for NGRI** but **substantially diminished** their responsibility

they are found **not guilty of murder but guilty of manslaughter** on the ground of diminished responsibility.

*s.6(1)*
