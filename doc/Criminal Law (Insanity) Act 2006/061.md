What is a patient on s.4 (committed after a finding of unfitness)?

**Unfit to be tried: committed.** Applies under **s.4(3)(b)(i)** (District Court) or **s.4(5)(c)(i)** (other courts).

- The court found the accused **unfit to be tried**, was satisfied they have a **mental disorder** and need **in-patient care**, and **committed** them to a designated centre **until an order is made under s.13**
- The **charge is still outstanding**: not convicted, not acquitted
- **Review Board** reviews at least every **6 months**; patient may also apply
- If they become **fit**, the clinical director notifies the **committing court**, and the patient is brought back before it
- If **still unfit** but no longer needing in-patient care, the Board may order further care or **discharge** (unconditional or conditional)
- Leave (temporary release) needs the **Minister's consent**
- **Part 4 of the MHA 2001** governs consent to treatment

*ss.4, 13, 14, 3(3)*
