What is a patient taken to hospital under s.14(8)?

**Removed to hospital for medical attention** not available in the designated centre.

- In **lawful custody** while there
- The clinical director must send a **report of the circumstances to the Minister within 48 hours** of the removal
- Separately, under **s.14(9)** the **Minister** may direct removal to a **specified place** in the interests of justice; the patient is then deemed in the **lawful custody of the designated centre**
