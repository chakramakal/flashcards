What is the Criminal Law (Insanity) Act 2006 mainly about?

- The **trial and detention** of people with a **mental disorder** who are charged with offences or found **not guilty by reason of insanity**
- **Unfitness to be tried** and the **special verdict**
- **Committal to designated centres**, including the Central Mental Hospital (CMH)
- **Independent review** of detention by the **Mental Health (Criminal Law) Review Board**
- **Diminished responsibility** (murder) and **transfer of prisoners** for treatment
