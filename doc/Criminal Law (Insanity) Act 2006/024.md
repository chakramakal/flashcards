What can the appeal court do on an NGRI appeal?

- **Not proved they did the act:** order **acquittal**
- **Not suffering from such a disorder:** **substitute a guilty verdict** (or manslaughter by diminished responsibility on a murder charge) with the same powers of sentencing
- **Ought to have been found unfit:** make that finding (s.4(5)(c) then applies)
- If they did have the disorder but would otherwise be guilty, it **dismisses the appeal**

*s.8(2)-(5), (7)-(10)*
