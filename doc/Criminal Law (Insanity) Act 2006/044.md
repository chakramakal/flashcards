When can a prisoner be transferred to a designated centre with their consent (s.15(1))?

When a **relevant officer** (an approved medical officer or a registered medical practitioner) **certifies in writing** that the prisoner has a **mental disorder** for which they **cannot be afforded appropriate care or treatment within the prison**, **and** the prisoner **voluntarily consents**, the **prison governor** may direct transfer in writing.

*s.15(1), (9)*
