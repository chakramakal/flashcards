Who decides fitness in the District Court, and what can it do if the accused is unfit?

For a **summary offence**, or an indictable offence being or to be tried **summarily**, the **District Court** decides. If unfit it **adjourns** and, having considered evidence from an **approved medical officer**, may:

- **commit** the person to a **designated centre** (if they have a mental disorder and need **in-patient** care) until an order is made under s.13, **or**
- make an order for **out-patient** care or treatment in a designated centre

If fit, the proceedings continue.
