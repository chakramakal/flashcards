What is the Mental Health (Criminal Law) Review Board?

An **independent** statutory body (An Bord Athbhreithnithe Meabhair-Shláinte (An Dlí Coiriúil)) that **reviews the detention** of people detained under this Act. In its work it must have regard to the **welfare and safety of the person** whose detention it reviews and to the **public interest**.

*s.11*
