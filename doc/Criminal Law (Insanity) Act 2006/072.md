What is a patient "unlawfully at large" (s.14(5))?

A patient on temporary release is **unlawfully at large** if the **release period has expired** or a **condition was broken**.

- A **Garda shall**, and a centre officer or servant **may**, **arrest without warrant** and bring them back
- If arrested, or they **return voluntarily**, after breaching a condition, the **release period is deemed to have expired**

*s.14(5)-(7)*
