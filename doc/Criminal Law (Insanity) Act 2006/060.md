Legal statuses at the CMH: what is each section called?

The Act describes these situations but does not give them names. These are working labels.

| Section | Status | Reviewed by |
|---|---|---|
| s.4(3)(b)(i), s.4(5)(c)(i) | **Unfit to be tried: committed** (in-patient) | Review Board |
| s.4(3)(b)(ii), s.4(5)(c)(ii) | **Unfit to be tried: out-patient order** | As the order sets out |
| s.4(6) | **Committed for examination** (fitness), up to 14 days | AMO reports to the court |
| s.5(2) | **NGRI: committed** | Review Board |
| s.5(3) | **NGRI: committed for examination**, up to 14 days, 6 months in total | AMO reports to the court |
| s.15(1) | **Prisoner transferred with consent** | Review Board |
| s.15(2) | **Prisoner transferred without consent** | Review Board |
| s.15(5)(a) | **Transferred prisoner kept after refusing treatment** | Review Board |
| s.13(5), (7) | **Conditionally discharged** | Per the Board's conditions |
| s.14(1) | **On temporary release** | Clinical director, with Minister's consent |
| MHA 2001 s.21(2) | **Civil patient transferred to the CMH** | Mental Health Tribunal |
| Defence Act ss.202, 203 | **Court-martial unfit / NGRI, committed** | Review Board |
