Which body reviews a patient's detention?

| Status | Reviewing body |
|---|---|
| s.4 unfit (committed) | **Review Board** |
| s.5 NGRI (committed) | **Review Board** |
| s.15 prisoner transferred | **Review Board** |
| Court-martial unfit / NGRI | **Review Board** |
| MHA 2001 patient (s.14 admission order, renewal order, s.21 transfer to CMH) | **Mental Health Tribunal** |
| Child under MHA s.25 | **District Court** (orders and extensions) |
