How do leave and transfer work in a designated centre compared with a civil approved centre?

| | Designated centre (2006 Act) | Approved centre (2001 Act) |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary release / leave | Clinical director, **with Minister's consent** (s.14(1)) | Responsible consultant psychiatrist, in writing (s.26) |
| Transfer | Clinical director with consent of **Minister, Minister for Health and other clinical director** (s.14(2)) | Clinical director with consent of the other clinical director (ss.20-21) |
| Absent without permission | **Unlawfully at large**; Garda **shall** arrest, centre staff **may** (s.14(5)-(7)) | Clinical director arranges return; Gardaí assist if serious risk (s.27) |
| Reviewing body | **Review Board** | **Mental Health Tribunal** |
| Medical attention elsewhere | Hospital; report to Minister within **48 h** (s.14(8)) | Hospital or other place (s.22) |
