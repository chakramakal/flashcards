How is a transferred prisoner's detention reviewed (s.17)?

- The Board reviews detention under s.15 at intervals of **not more than 6 months**
- The **prisoner may apply** for a review; the Board may also review on its **own initiative**
- The **Minister** may direct a review where the detention follows a certification **without consent** (s.15(2) or (5)(a))
- If the prisoner still has the disorder and cannot be treated in prison, the Board **refuses to order transfer back**; otherwise, after **consulting the Minister**, it orders transfer **back to prison** (or another prison)
