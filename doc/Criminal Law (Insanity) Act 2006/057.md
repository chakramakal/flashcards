Key time limits under the Criminal Law (Insanity) Act 2006.

| Item | Limit | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Committal for examination (unfit or NGRI) | up to 14 days | 4(6), 5(3) |
| Extensions of NGRI examination committal | total not over 6 months | 5(3) |
| Review of detention | at least every 6 months | 13(2), 17(2) |
| Report to Minister after hospital removal | within 48 hours | 14(8) |
| Notice of intention to adduce mental-condition evidence | within 10 days of plea being asked | 19 |
| Review Board member's term | 5 years | Sch. 1 |
