What is the test for being unfit to be tried?

An accused is **unfit** if, **by reason of mental disorder**, they cannot **understand the nature or course of the proceedings** so as to:

- **plead** to the charge
- **instruct a legal representative**
- **elect for jury trial** (indictable offence triable summarily)
- **make a proper defence**
- **challenge a juror** they might wish to object to
- **understand the evidence**

*s.4(2)*
