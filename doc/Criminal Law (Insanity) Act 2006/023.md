What are the grounds of appeal against an NGRI finding?

The person may appeal on any of these grounds:

- it was **not proved they committed the act**
- they were **not suffering** from a mental disorder of the kind in s.5(1)(b) at the time
- the court **ought to have found them unfit** to be tried

To the **Circuit Court** (District Court cases) or the **Court of Criminal Appeal** (trial on indictment in the Circuit, Central or Special Criminal Court).

*s.8(1), (6)*
