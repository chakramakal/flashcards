Who decides fitness for more serious offences?

- The **District Court sends the accused forward** to the court of trial, where the question is decided by the **judge sitting alone** (no jury)
- In any court other than the District Court, the **judge sitting alone** decides
- If **unfit**, the court **adjourns** and may commit (in-patient) or make an out-patient order, as above
- If **fit**, the proceedings continue

*s.4(4)-(5)*
