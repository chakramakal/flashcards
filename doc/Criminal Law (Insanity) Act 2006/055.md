Compare the legal statuses of people detained under this Act.

| | Unfit to be tried (s.4) | NGRI (s.5) | Transferred prisoner (s.15) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Court finding | Unfit to be tried | Committed the act, **not responsible** (special verdict) | None: not a court finding |
| Authority | **Court committal** | **Court committal** | **Prison governor's direction** (on certification) |
| Consent needed? | No | No | **Consent**, or certification by **2+ relevant officers** |
| Reviewed by | **Review Board**, at least every **6 months** | **Review Board**, at least every **6 months** | **Review Board**, at least every **6 months** |
| If better | Back to **committing court** for trial, or Board orders further care or discharge | Board orders further care or **discharge** (conditional or not) | **Transferred back to prison** |
