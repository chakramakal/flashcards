What if it is unclear whether an NGRI person needs in-patient care?

- The court may commit them to a designated centre for **up to 14 days** for examination by an **approved medical officer**
- On application by any party, and after consulting an AMO, it may **extend** the period, but the **total must not exceed 6 months**
- The AMO reports within the period on whether they have a mental disorder and need in-patient care

*s.5(3)*
