Define "mental disorder" under this Act (s.1).

Includes **mental illness, mental disability, dementia or any disease of the mind**. It does **not** include **intoxication**.

For a **committal** (in-patient care in a designated centre), the court must also be satisfied the person has a mental disorder **within the meaning of the Mental Health Act 2001** and needs in-patient care.
