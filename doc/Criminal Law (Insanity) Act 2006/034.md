What if an unfit patient is still unfit but no longer needs in-patient care (s.13(4)-(5))?

- The clinical director must **forthwith notify the Review Board**
- The Board orders the patient to be **brought before it**, hears evidence from the **responsible consultant psychiatrist**, and decides whether treatment is still required
- It may order **further detention, care or treatment** in a designated centre, **or discharge** (unconditionally, or on **conditions for out-patient treatment or supervision or both**)
