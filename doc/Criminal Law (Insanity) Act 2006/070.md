What is a conditionally discharged patient?

**Conditionally discharged.** After a review under **s.13**, the Review Board may discharge a patient **subject to conditions for out-patient treatment or supervision or both**.

- Living outside the designated centre under the **Board's conditions**
- The Act text provided here does **not** set out recall or breach procedures, so check the **specific order** and ask the CNM

*s.13(5), (7), (8)(b), (9)*
