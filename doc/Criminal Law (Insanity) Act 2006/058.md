Who decides what? Roles under the 2006 Act.

| Role | Decision |
|---|---|
| Court (judge or jury) | Fitness to be tried; NGRI verdict; diminished responsibility; committal |
| Approved medical officer | Evidence on mental disorder and need for in-patient care; reports on 14-day or 6-month examination committals |
| Clinical director | Notifies court or Review Board when no longer unfit or no longer needing in-patient care; temporary release and transfer (with Minister's consent) |
| Review Board | Reviews detention at least every 6 months; orders further care, discharge or return to court |
| Minister | Consents to release and transfer; directs removal; consulted on transfer back to prison |
| Prison governor | Directs transfer of a prisoner on certification |
