What is a patient on temporary release (s.14(1))?

**On temporary release.**

- The **clinical director** directed release, **with the consent of the Minister**, on conditions and for a period they consider appropriate
- Conditions must be given to the patient **in writing** at the time
- The patient **must comply**
- Section 14 refers to "patients", meaning anyone detained in a designated centre under this Act
- Contrast **s.26 of the MHA 2001**, where the consultant psychiatrist alone grants leave

*s.14(1), (3)-(4)*
