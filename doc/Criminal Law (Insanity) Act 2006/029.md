What are a patient's procedural rights at a Review Board review?

- **Notice** (patient, legal representative and responsible consultant psychiatrist) of the date, time and place
- A **copy of any document** furnished to the Board and an indication of the nature and source of other information that came to notice
- To be **present** and present their case **in person or through a legal representative**
- To **cross-examine** witnesses called by the Board and to call their own
- **Written statements** are admissible only with their (or their legal representative's) consent

*s.12(6)*
