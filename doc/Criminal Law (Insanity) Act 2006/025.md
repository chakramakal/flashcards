Can committal orders be appealed (s.9)?

Yes. The **defence or the prosecution** may appeal a decision by the court of trial to make, or not make, an **order of committal** under s.4 or s.5 to the **Circuit Court** or **Court of Criminal Appeal**, which may make any order the trial court could have made. **No further appeal**, but the detention is still subject to **Review Board** review under s.13.
