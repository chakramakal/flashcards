What does the 2006 Act provide for?

The **special verdict** of 'not guilty by reason of insanity' (NGRI), findings of **unfitness to be tried**, the defence of **diminished responsibility** (murder only), detention in a **designated centre** (the CMH), and the **Mental Health (Criminal Law) Review Board**. Amended by the Criminal Law (Insanity) Act 2010.
