# Define 'mental disorder' (s.3).

- **Mental illness, severe dementia or significant intellectual disability** where:
- (a) serious likelihood of **immediate and serious harm** to self/others; OR
- (b)(i) judgment so impaired that not admitting would likely cause **serious deterioration** or prevent treatment only available by admission AND (ii) admission would likely **benefit or alleviate** the condition to a material extent.

*s.3(1)*
