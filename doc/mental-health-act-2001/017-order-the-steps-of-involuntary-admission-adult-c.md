# Order the steps of involuntary admission (adult, community route).

- 1 **Application** to an RMP
- 2 RMP **examines** within 24 h and makes a **recommendation** (valid 7 days)
- 3 Removal to approved centre
- 4 **Consultant psychiatrist** on the centre's staff examines and makes an **admission order** (within 24 h)
- 5 Notice to patient and copy to Commission within 24 h
- 6 Commission refers to **tribunal**, assigns legal rep, independent psychiatrist reports
- 7 Tribunal decides within 21 days
